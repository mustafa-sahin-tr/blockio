Delete your Blockio: Time Rush account and data

How to request deletion

  1. Open Blockio: Time Rush → Profile. Note your in-game name (if you set one).
  2. Send an email to mtafasahin@gmail.com with the subject "Blockio account deletion". Include your in-game name and the platform (Android or iOS). If you linked Google Play Games or Sign in with Apple, mention it.
  3. We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

You can also uninstall the app: progress stored only on your device is removed with it.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous player account (Unity Authentication player ID) and any linked Google Play Games / Apple sign-in.
  • Your cloud-saved progress, scores and leaderboard entries, and your in-game display name.
  • Redeemed gift-code records tied to your player ID.
  • Anonymous gameplay analytics events linked to your player ID.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play / the Apple App Store under their own policies; we do not store payment details.
  • Aggregated, anonymous statistics that no longer identify you may be kept.
  • Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob under Google's policies.
  • Everything else is removed within 30 days of your request.

Delete only some data

You can ask us to delete only part of your data (for example your display name or leaderboard entries) without deleting the account. Send the same email and say what you want removed. You can also turn off anonymous gameplay data any time in Settings.
