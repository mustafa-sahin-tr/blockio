    Game rules

    The core rules, goals, special cells, power-ups and every look the board can take.

    Board and modes

    The board is 8×8 squares. The tray offers three pieces at a time; drag one onto empty squares. When all three are placed, three new ones arrive. There is no gravity: a block stays exactly where you put it.

    In Adventure every level has goals and a limited number of moves; complete the goals to win. Power-ups, landmarks and rewards all live in Adventure. Classic has no goals: you chase your best score. There is no undo.

    Pieces

    Pieces can't be rotated; they go down the way the tray offers them (unless you use the Rotate power-up). Every orientation counts as its own piece. Pieces belong to families, and some goals ask for a piece from a given family.

    Clearing lines

    A row or column clears as soon as it is completely full. Clearing several lines in one move, or on back-to-back moves, scores more. When none of the tray pieces fits on the board, the run is over.

    Special cells

    Some levels put special cells on the board. Each one behaves in its own way, and knowing how they work makes the goal much easier.

    • Explosive Core

      Born from a row+column clear. When it blows, it clears the 3×3 around it, its row and its column.

    • Vortex

      Earned every 5 line clears (later on hard levels). Destroying it fills the board's enclosed gaps.

    • Coin Cell

      Pays coins when cleared, double where a row and a column cross.

    • Timer Block

      Drops by 1 each move; clear it before 0. In Path mode, running out ends the run.

    • Hold pocket

      Drag a piece into the pocket to keep it. Drop it on a full pocket and the two swap.

    • Locked Chest

      Opens once enough of its different neighbours are cleared, then spills gold: as many coins as a Coin cell.

    • Power Star

      Line clears through it don't remove it — each adds a charge. At 3 charges it bursts, clearing the 3×3 around it.

    • Puzzle Link

      Locked pieces only come off if every one is hit in the same move, and then pay bonus points. Hit just one and none goes.

    • Reinforced Group

      A 2x2 or 3x3 block with one shared counter. Every row or column cleared along it or through it knocks off one item; when the last one goes, the whole group empties at once.

    • Arrow Box

      When its row or column is cleared, it moves one square in the arrow's direction. If the square ahead is taken, it waits. Once it is on the edge, one more hit takes it off the board.

    • Colour Crate

      Each item inside has a block colour. Clear a row or column so that a block of that colour touches the crate and that item breaks; other colours only shake it. Power-ups break a random item. When the last one goes, the whole crate empties.

    Power-ups

    Power-ups exist only in Adventure. You earn them from levels, badges and rewarded ads, and some can also be bought with coins in the shop.

    • Bomb

      Drag the icon onto the board and let go: the 3x3 area under it clears instantly.

      50

    • Row Clear

      Drag it onto the board and let go over a row: that row clears from end to end, full or not.

      50

    • Column Clear

      Drag it onto the board and let go over a column: that column clears from end to end, full or not.

      50

    • Joker

      Tap an empty cell: it fills in, and any row or column it completes clears.

      10

    • Colour Cleanser

      Tap a filled cell: every block of that colour on the board disappears.

      75

    • Rotate

      Tap a dock piece to turn it 90°. Each tap turns it another quarter.

      50

    • Refresh

      Swaps all three dock pieces for new ones — at least one is sure to fit.

      75

    • 2x Multiplier

      For 15 seconds, every point you score is doubled.

      200

    • Coin Sower

      Sows coin cells onto the board at level start; clear them to earn coins.

      Not sold in the shop

    • H

      Hold pocket

      Drag a piece into the pocket to keep it. Drop it on a full pocket and the two swap.

      Not sold in the shop

    Board shapes

    Some levels are played on a board with a different outline instead of the full square. Closed squares can't hold pieces, so every shape asks for a new plan.
