    World wonders

    The wonders of the ancient world are special landmarks. A new wonder opens every 6 regions. Finished wonders pay a daily bonus of 25 coins.

    Stage 1 · 300Stage 2 · 450Stage 3 · 600

    • Temple of Artemis Wonder

      İzmir · Ephesus

      The Temple of Artemis at Ephesus is one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. Today only its foundations and pieces remain. Source: Wikipedia

    • Mausoleum at Halicarnassus Wonder

      Bodrum · Fethiye

      The Mausoleum at Halicarnassus was one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. The word ‘mausoleum’ comes from its owner, Mausolus. Source: Wikipedia

    • Great Pyramid of Giza Wonder

      The oldest of the Seven Wonders and the only one still standing. It was built for the pharaoh Khufu about 4,500 years ago. Source: Wikipedia

    • Lighthouse of Alexandria Wonder

      This lighthouse on the island of Pharos guided ships into port. For centuries it was one of the tallest buildings in the world. Source: Wikipedia

    • Colossus of Rhodes Wonder

      A bronze statue of the sun god Helios, about 33 metres tall. An earthquake brought it down about half a century after it was raised. Source: Wikipedia

    • Statue of Zeus at Olympia Wonder

      Made by the sculptor Phidias from ivory and gold, this seated statue of Zeus stood in his temple at Olympia. Source: Wikipedia

    • Hanging Gardens of Babylon Wonder

      Described as lush gardens rising in terraces. Exactly where they stood is still debated today. Source: Wikipedia
