    Blockio: Time Rush

    Blockio: Time Rush is a block puzzle. Place pieces on the board, fill rows and columns, and watch them clear. In Adventure you travel level by level from Türkiye around the world, build landmark models and collect gems and badges. Classic is a calm score game.
