    USA

    The USA route: 3 regions, 12 landmarks. Every landmark is built in three stages.

    New York

    Route steps 1–12

    • Statue of Liberty

      A gift from France in 1886, the statue was shiny copper-brown at first. The copper slowly turned green in the air. Source: Wikipedia

    • Empire State Building

      The 102-storey building went up in 1930 to 1931 and stayed the world's tallest building for nearly 40 years. Source: Wikipedia

    • Brooklyn Bridge

      Opened on 24 May 1883, it was the first fixed crossing of the East River. It hangs from steel-wire cables. Source: Wikipedia

    • Central Park

      Central Park covers 341 hectares. It is the most visited city park in the USA, with 42 million visitors in 2016. Source: Wikipedia

    Washington, D.C.

    Route steps 13–24

    • Lincoln Memorial

      The memorial is ringed by 36 columns, one for each of the 36 states in the country when Lincoln died. Source: Wikipedia

    • Washington Monument

      This 169-metre obelisk was the world's tallest structure from 1884 to 1889, until the Eiffel Tower passed it. Source: Wikipedia

    • Jefferson Memorial

      Built from 1939 to 1943, the memorial was inspired by Rome's Pantheon. Its bronze statue of Jefferson was added in 1947. Source: Wikipedia

    • Tidal Basin Cherry Blossoms

      The first 3,020 cherry trees here were a gift sent from the city of Tokyo to Washington in 1912. Source: Wikipedia

    Natural Wonders

    Route steps 25–36

    • Niagara Falls

      Niagara Falls is really three waterfalls. The biggest, Horseshoe Falls, sits right on the border of the USA and Canada. Source: Wikipedia

    • Yellowstone Geyser

      Yellowstone's Old Faithful geyser is very regular: it erupts about every 92 minutes on average. Source: Wikipedia

    • Yosemite's Half Dome

      Half Dome rises over 2,680 metres above sea level. Hikers climb the last stretch holding on to two steel cables. Source: Wikipedia

    • Giant Sequoias

      The giant sequoia called General Sherman is the largest known tree on Earth by volume. It is about 84 metres tall. Source: Wikipedia
