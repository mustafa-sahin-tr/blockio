    Vietnam

    The Vietnam route: 3 regions, 12 landmarks. Every landmark is built in three stages.

    Sa Pa

    Route steps 1–12

    • Rice Terraces

      The Mường Hoa Valley, between Sa Pa and Mount Fansipan, is famous for rice fields cut like steps into the hills. Source: Wikipedia

    • Mount Fansipan Cable Car

      At 3,147 metres, Fansipan is the highest mountain in Indochina, so it is nicknamed the ‘Roof of Indochina’. Source: Wikipedia

    • Cát Cát Village

      Cát Cát is a Hmong village founded in the mid-1800s. Villagers weave colourful cloth dyed with indigo. Source: Wikipedia

    • Silver Waterfall

      The Silver Waterfall is over 200 metres high. Its name comes from the bright white foam of the falling water. Source: Wikipedia

    Hanoi

    Route steps 13–24

    • Hoàn Kiếm Lake and Turtle Tower

      The lake's name means ‘Lake of the Returned Sword’. Legend says a golden turtle took back Emperor Lê Lợi's magic sword here. Source: Wikipedia

    • Huc Bridge

      The bridge was built in 1865 to reach Ngọc Sơn Temple on the lake. Its name means ‘the rising and perching sun’. Source: Wikipedia

    • Temple of Literature

      Founded in 1070, the temple was home to Vietnam's first national university, the Imperial Academy, from 1076. Source: Wikipedia

    • Old Quarter Streets

      The Old Quarter is also called the ‘36 streets’. In the past, each street had its own trade or craft. Source: Wikipedia

    Hạ Long Bay

    Route steps 25–36

    • Limestone Islands

      Hạ Long Bay has about 1,969 islets, most of them limestone. It became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1994. Source: Wikipedia

    • Traditional Junk Boat

      Junk boats are flat-bottomed, with hulls split into watertight rooms. Their sails were once woven from bamboo. Source: Wikipedia

    • Sửng Sốt Cave

      The cave's entrance is tiny, but inside it opens up wide. In 1901 the French named it the ‘Cave of Surprise’. Source: Wikipedia

    • Floating Fishing Village

      About 1,600 people live in four fishing villages on Hạ Long Bay, in floating houses, and live by fishing. Source: Wikipedia
